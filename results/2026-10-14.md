# Initial OpenCode sweep, 2026-10-14

OpenCode 1.19.0, Pentect 0.0.95, Windows, OpenRouter. The same four scenarios
were attempted three times per model, directly and through Pentect. Each
attempt used a new synthetic secret, isolated project, and client home. The
relay recorded whether the exact secret appeared in an outbound model request.
The test does not measure general coding or reasoning capability.
The [value-free per-model reports](raw-2026-10-14) are included for audit;
they were produced by the original runner before this standalone repository
added binary hashes and timestamps to newly generated reports.

The denominators include completed pairs only. Kimi had only one completed
log pair and cannot be ranked. Direct exposure may be lower when a model
chooses a local shell copy that never sends the value to the model. That does
not make a Pentect route safer or less safe by itself.

Pentect-route exposure occurred for Qwen3.8 Flash, GPT-5 mini, and GLM 5.3
Flash. These are observed failures of the tested client/gateway/model routes.
The value-free report does not isolate the root cause. A model with 0 observed
exposures in this small sweep is not certified safe. File-copy completion and
privacy must be reported separately: GPT-4o mini and GPT-4o had no Pentect
file-copy successes under the strict exact-value criterion.
